3. Profiles, discovery, and who can see your information

Om shows active profiles using the details people provide and the filters available in the app. You choose whom to invite to a video call or message. A video call connects only after the other person accepts the invitation. We do not promise profile accuracy or a particular match.

Other Om users may see your name, username, age, country, language, gender, bio, profile/gallery photos, online status, and Pro or verification badges. Your email and exact birth date are not public profile fields. Anything another user sees can be copied or captured outside Om; share carefully.

4. Selfie and face verification

Verification is optional. If you request a verified badge, Om captures a still selfie using the front camera and sends it to Om’s API. The API retrieves your current profile photo from private photo storage, resizes both images, and sends the images to Anthropic’s API. An automated image model compares whether the two images appear to show the same adult person and returns a verification decision.

The selfie is not saved to your Om profile, Firestore, or photo bucket by the verification flow. Om stores the resulting verification status on your profile; it does not create or store a faceprint, face embedding, or reusable biometric template. Facial imagery can nevertheless be treated as biometric or sensitive personal information under some laws, so we describe the comparison plainly. A badge is not proof of legal identity or a safety check.

Anthropic processes the images as an API service provider. Under Anthropic’s standard API retention policy, API inputs and outputs are deleted from its backend within 30 days, with exceptions for legal obligations, safety/Usage Policy enforcement, or other stated service arrangements. See Anthropic’s API data-retention information.

We also send profile text and profile/gallery images to Anthropic’s API for automated content safety moderation. The API returns a moderation decision; Om uses it to approve or reject content and does not use it to create an advertising profile.

5. Messages, notifications, and live video

Messages

Messages and conversation records are stored in Cloud Firestore so Om can show them to the participants and deliver requests, unread counts, and read status. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; Om’s service and authorized service providers can process them to deliver the feature, respond to reports, protect users, or meet legal obligations.

A Pro sender can create an initial message request; the recipient does not need Pro. The first message may be shown with the request, and the recipient must accept before an ongoing chat begins. If you submit a report, you may choose to include a message and report details.

Video and audio

Live video and audio are transmitted through LiveKit to connect the two participants. Om’s current app does not enable LiveKit end-to-end encryption and does not run a call-recording feature. If you tap Report during a call, the app captures a screenshot of the current screen and attaches it to the report you submit. A participant may also use device-level recording outside Om; do not share anything you would not want captured.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, Om stores your device push token and uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to deliver call invitations, message requests, and message alerts. An alert may show a sender’s name and a message preview on your lock screen. You can turn notifications off or hide previews in iOS Settings. Notifications are optional and are not required to use Om.

8. Retention and account deletion

We keep profile and account information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Messages and conversations remain until a participant deletes the conversation or the records are no longer needed. We keep reports and any attached screenshots while needed to investigate safety concerns, enforce rules, resolve disputes, or comply with law. Technical logs are retained for security and troubleshooting according to our hosting and operational settings. Some providers may retain backup copies for a limited period under their own retention schedules.

What account deletion currently removes: deleting your account in Om deletes the sign-in account, public profile document, linked sign-in mapping, and registered push tokens. It does not automatically erase existing messages/conversations, reports and report evidence, block/favorite records, call-invitation metadata, or uploaded photo files in object storage. Those records may remain after the public profile is removed. The current service has no published fixed automatic purge period for all such retained records.

To request review or deletion of retained information, email mehmetsulu3535@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and include your Om username or user ID. We will assess the request and delete or de-identify information where appropriate, subject to safety, legal, and dispute-resolution needs. Deleting the app does not delete your account or cancel an Apple subscription.

You can delete an individual conversation from the Messages screen. That action removes the conversation and its messages for both participants. You can update profile fields in the app. You can also withdraw camera/microphone, photo, or notification permissions in iOS Settings; without camera/microphone access, video calls or selfie verification cannot work.
